    <h1>About Me</h1>
    <p class="intro-lead">Hello, I’m <strong>Xing Hu</strong>.</p>

    <p>My primary areas of research interest encompass <strong>Empirical Industrial Organization, International Trade, and Applied Econometrics</strong>.</p>
